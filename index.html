(02) The Trainer

Rajender
Chauhan

“Wolfmaster Bunty”

Over two decades, Rajender Chauhan has become one of the best-known names in Indian dog training, with a reputation that reaches well beyond the country's borders.

He has trained dogs professionally since 2002. As founder and director of Wolfmaster K9 he works as a canine behaviourist, a Protection Sports Association competitor and a PSA decoy. He has also brought international-standard protection sport to India by hosting PSA trials and decoy certification camps.

His method is uncompromising. Every dog must be clear, confident and completely under control, whether it is guarding a family home or working on the trial field.

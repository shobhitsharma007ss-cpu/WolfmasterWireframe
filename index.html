The Man Behind The Name

Rajender Chauhan.
Wolfmaster Bunty.

Rajender Chauhan, known across India's dog community as Wolfmaster Bunty, has spent more than two decades building working dogs. He has trained dogs since 2002. As founder and director of Wolfmaster K9, he has become one of the best-known names in Indian dog training, with a reputation that extends well beyond India.

He works as a dog behaviourist, Protection Sports Association (PSA) competitor and PSA decoy, and he has brought international-standard protection sport to India by hosting PSA trials and decoy certification camps. His approach is simple. A dog should be clear, confident and completely under control, whether it is guarding a family or competing on the field.
